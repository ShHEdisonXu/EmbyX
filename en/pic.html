About

EmbyX

v2.0

Bookmark the world, cherish every frame.

👋 Hi, I am @Juneix. EmbyX is a TikTok-style web player for Emby / Jellyfin. Experience your private media library in a whole new, immersive way. 🎉

All data stays on your device. Free to use.

Photo Gallery

  • Only items indexed by Emby as photos are displayed.
  • Live Photo companion videos and regular videos are ignored.

🧩 Tips & Tricks

  • Use an Emby photo library for predictable filtering.
  • Smoother Loading: Keep each photo library reasonably sized.
  • Easier Managing: Use multiple libraries and playlists.
  • PWA Support: Add to Home Screen or Install as App. 📲
